The CSV files in this folder are the dataset's two original CSV tables. The
dataset describes itself as synthetic. MediBed uses the updated table only
to create scaled, fictional demo bed inventories and illustrative daily
history. Assigned capacities, availability, equipment attributes and forecasts
are not reported by or verified with any hospital.

The seed transformation is documented in seed.py. MIT license text is in
LICENSE-KAGGLE-HOSPITAL-BEDS.txt.
