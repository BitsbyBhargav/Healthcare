Privacy & service information.

Last updated: 18 November 2026

What this service does

MediBed is an independent Pune hospital directory with a pilot inventory and request workspace. Real hospital names and published contact numbers are sourced from official pages. Listed institutions have not been onboarded as participating hospitals. Your workspace requests are not sent to them and are not medical admissions.

Your account information

Registration stores your name, email address, a one-way password hash and creation time. Requests store the selected hospital, bed category, equipment requirements, priority and status. Notifications and workspace changes are stored in the application's database. Do not enter medical records, diagnoses, identity documents or clinical information.

Access and storage

You can view your requests after sign-in. The administrator assigned to the selected workspace can see your name and request requirements to process the internal request. Account and request data are retained in the application database and any operator-created backups until deletion by the operator. Email ownership verification and automated account deletion are not currently available.

Sessions and security

A signed session cookie supports sign-in. No advertising cookies or analytics services are used. Authentication rate limiting temporarily stores keyed hashes of IP addresses; request access logs may also contain IP addresses at the hosting provider. Hosting infrastructure handles ordinary connection information.

Telephone calls

Call buttons open your device's dialer or configured calling application. MediBed does not record conversations, place calls automatically, transmit your location, dispatch a vehicle or confirm an ETA. The dispatcher must confirm transport arrangements.
